Game Controls & Rules

⚔️ Game Modes

  • Solo Mode: Battle against the intelligent Computer AI.
  • 2-Player Mode: Play against a friend locally on the same device with simultaneous secret lock-ins!
  • Online Duel: Play in real time against another player over the internet with shareable links and live chat!

⌨️ Solo & Online Keyboard

  • 1 or R — Rock
  • 2 or P — Paper
  • 3 or S — Scissors

👥 2-Player Local Dual Keyboard

Player 1:

  • A = Rock | S = Paper | D = Scissors

Player 2:

  • J or ← = Rock
  • K or ↑ = Paper
  • L or → = Scissors

✨ Universal Keys

  • M — Toggle Audio Effects
  • Space — Reset Match
  • Esc — Close Modals

⚙️ Cloud Multiplayer Configuration

This game uses Firebase Realtime Database to synchronize online rooms, moves, scores, and live chat across devices.

Database Status: Checking connection...

Quick Firebase Console Setup (3 Steps):

  1. Go to console.firebase.google.com and create or select your project.
  2. Click Build → Realtime Database → Create Database (Select Test Mode: .read: true, .write: true).
  3. Go to Project Settings (gear icon) → General → Your apps → Web (</>) and paste the firebaseConfig object below.

Ensure your databaseURL ends with .firebaseio.com or .firebasedatabase.app, and your Realtime Database Rules allow read/write.

Optional: paste your live Vercel URL when testing locally so shared invite links open on other devices.
